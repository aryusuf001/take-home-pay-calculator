# Take Home Pay Calculator

Live Application: [https://takehomepaycalc.me/](https://takehomepaycalc.me/)

A suite of net income and salary calculation tools designed to help individuals calculate accurate take-home pay across different regions, tax years, and employment sectors.

---

* **Main Calculator**: [Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/)

  * [Ireland Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/ireland-take-home-pay-calculator)
  * [London Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/london-take-home-pay-calculator)
  * [Northern Ireland Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/ni-take-home-pay-calculator)
  * [Scotland Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/scotland-take-home-pay-calculator)
* **Sector-Specific**:
  * [NHS Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/nhs-take-home-pay-calculator)

* **Specialized Calculations**:
  * [Monthly Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/monthly-take-home-pay-calculator)
  * [Student Loan Take Home Pay Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/student-loan-take-home-pay-calculator)
  * [2025–2026 Tax Year Calculator](https://takehomepaycalc.me/take-home-pay-calculator-2025-2026)

---

## Features

* **Multi-Region Tax Rules**: Deductions tailored for UK regions (Scotland, Northern Ireland, London) and Ireland.
* **Sector Deductions**: Specific tax and pension configurations for NHS staff and teachers.
* **Student Loans**: Precise calculations factoring in active student loan repayments.
* **Flexible Intervals**: Break down earnings on a monthly or annual basis.
